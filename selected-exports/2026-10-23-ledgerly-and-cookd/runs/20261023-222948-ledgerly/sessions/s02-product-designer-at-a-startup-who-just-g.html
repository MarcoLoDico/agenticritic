    Expected: Some sign that my change mattered, or a clear message about what to fix. · Happened: The identical 'Invalid input' banner and another full wipe. · Why it matters: This was my third failed attempt, and I had no clue whether the date, the cost center, the category or the missing receipt was the cause.   • step 17: My expense list contained only AWS and Delta, both already Approved, and nothing I submitted.
    Expected: A list of my own expenses with a status like Pending or Approved. · Happened: Two company-looking entries that aren't mine. My $240 ticket wasn't there because it never saved, and nothing told me that. · Why it matters: I can't tell what happened to my expense. I don't know if it's pending, lost, or never sent.
  • step 18: The expense detail page shows only amount, date, category and 'Approved (auto)'.
    Expected: Who submitted it, who approved it, whether there's a receipt, and what happens next. · Happened: A bare page with no submitter, no receipt and no next step. 'Auto' isn't explained. · Why it matters: I can't learn how approval works or who my approver is. I also can't find where to attach a receipt to an existing expense.

Missing features

  • Receipt photo upload (camera or file) on the new expense form and on existing expenses — Getting paid back means proving what I spent. This is the main thing I came to do and I couldn't find it anywhere.
  • Field-level error messages that keep what I typed — A generic 'Invalid input' with a wiped form makes it impossible to correct the problem, and it's miserable on a phone.
  • Plain-language help or a dropdown for Cost center code and Category — I don't know finance terms and can't tell what to pick for a conference ticket.
  • A clear confirmation after submitting, saying who approves it and when I'll get paid — I want to know what happens next so I can stop thinking about it.
  • A 'My expenses' view with status, approver and submitter — I need to check whether my own expense was approved without seeing other people's company spend.
  • An employee path that skips finance setup — Connecting accounting software isn't something I know about or should be asked to do.

Liked

  • The dashboard did have a clear 'Submit an expense' button that I found quickly.
  • The new expense form is short, with only five fields.
  • 'Remind me later' let me skip the accounting setup, though it was easy to miss.
