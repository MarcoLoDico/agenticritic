Missing features

  • Receipt photo upload (camera or file) on the New expense form and on existing expenses — Snapping a receipt is the first thing I expect to do, and I couldn't find it anywhere.
  • Inline, field-specific validation that keeps what I typed — I need to see exactly which field is wrong and not lose my input, especially on a phone.
  • Plain-language help for Category and Cost center (examples, defaults, or an 'I don't know' option) — I don't know finance terms, and a wrong guess shouldn't block me.
  • Confirmation after submit saying what happens next, who approves it, and when I get paid back — I want to be told what happens next and whether it went through.
  • An employee-friendly start that skips admin setup, and a 'my expenses' view with clear pending/approved/rejected status — I'm not the admin, and I need to find my own claims and see whether they're approved.

Liked

  • The 'Submit an expense' button on the dashboard was clear plain English and easy to find.
  • The accounting connection step had a Skip option, so I could get past it.
  • The Expenses list showed a clear 'Approved' status.
