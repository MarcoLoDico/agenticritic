Missing features

  • Setup checklist on the dashboard (accounting, bank details, invites, cards) with done/not-done status — I need the product to tell me plainly what's still needed before cards work.
  • Obvious 'Invite people' button on the dashboard or a top-level Team page, using plain wording and an email field — Inviting my team should take a few clicks. It took three levels of menus.
  • Card confirmation screen showing status, and the ability to freeze or delete a card — I need to know a card is usable, and to fix a duplicate I created by accident.
  • Duplicate-card warning for the same cardholder — I accidentally gave Marcus two cards.
  • Cardholder picker listing existing team members instead of a free-text name — Typing a name is error-prone, and the card isn't tied to Marcus's real account.
  • Clear 'sample data' labeling, or an option to clear demo expenses — I don't recognize the AWS and Delta expenses on my dashboard.

Liked

  • 'Remind me later' on the accounting step let me skip homework, once I found it.
  • The card form was short, with a sensible $1000 default limit.
  • Adding seats was fast once I found the page: type an email, click, and it shows in the table with an activation-email message.
  • The dashboard 'Issue a card' button was easy to spot.
