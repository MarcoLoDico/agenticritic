  • Card detail page with edit, freeze, controls and transactions — I need to inspect and manage a card after issuing it, and confirm the limit works.
  • Public API, API keys and webhooks — I want to automate card creation and get spend events into our own tooling. I found no sign of an API anywhere.
  • Documented roles and permissions, ideally with 'view as' or a role preview — I need to know what Submitter, Approver and Admin can do before I give them access.
  • Working accounting integration with a clear error and retry path — The QuickBooks connection failed twice, and the error gave me nothing to act on.
  • Card nickname or purpose field, separate from the cardholder — The card list needs readable names once there are several tool cards.
  • Search or command menu and keyboard shortcuts — I never found a way to jump between settings, cards and members quickly, which I expect from a modern tool.
  • Form draft persistence or an unsaved-changes warning — I lost my card form when I navigated to Settings.
  • SSO and identity federation details — The tab exists under Workspace Administration, but I never opened it and the plans page puts SSO on Scale with no pricing.
